pdsynth factory bank, 27 voices in 2 files
Each file is one CZ internal memory area: send it and it fills
Internal 1 to 16. MIDI channel 1.

A CZ has no velocity, no stereo field and no mod wheel, so every
voice plays more plainly on the hardware than it does in pdsynth.
That is true of all of them and is not repeated below.
"Approximate" means something that changes the voice itself is
missing from the CZ's side of it.
